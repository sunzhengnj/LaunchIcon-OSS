# Support

## Bugs and crashes

Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/sunzhengnj/LaunchIcon-OSS/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Provide the installed app version, macOS version, chip, and reproduction steps. Do not upload full layout files, preference files, or unsanitized crash logs.

## Downloads

Installers and checksums are published on [Releases](https://github.com/sunzhengnj/LaunchIcon-OSS/releases). Prefer the latest release unless you are bisecting a regression.

- Product scope and priorities: [Docs/LaunchIcon_PRD.md](Docs/LaunchIcon_PRD.md)
- How public updates relate to private milestone development: [Docs/SYNC_FROM_PRIVATE.md](Docs/SYNC_FROM_PRIVATE.md)
